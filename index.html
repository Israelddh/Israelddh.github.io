Profile

Abstract

Biomedical scientist with a background in Biology, Bioinformatics, and Biostatistics, and four years of experience in clinical and translational research. My work combines scientific literature, biomedical data, statistical analysis, and scientific communication.

I have contributed to peer-reviewed research through manuscript preparation, interpretation of clinical and multi-omic data, scientific reporting, data visualization, and collaboration with clinicians, epidemiologists, and bioinformaticians. I am also the first author or co-author of eight peer-reviewed publications and have reviewed four manuscripts for international biomedical journals.1

I am now focusing these skills on Scientific and Medical Writing: researching and evaluating evidence, understanding the science behind the data, and communicating it clearly to the audience that needs it. My aim is to combine the scientific rigor of a researcher with the clarity, structure, and audience awareness expected from a professional writer.
