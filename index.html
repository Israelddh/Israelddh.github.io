Profile

Abstract

Healthcare Data Analyst and Biomedical Scientist with a background in Biology, Bioinformatics, and Biostatistics, combining clinical research expertise with hands-on experience in analysing, interpreting, and communicating complex biomedical data. I work with clinical, epidemiological, and multi-omic datasets, with a focus on data quality, statistical analysis, reproducible workflows, and turning complex data into reliable evidence and clear, decision-ready reporting.

First author and co-author of eight peer-reviewed publications, with experience as a peer reviewer for international biomedical journals. My background in scientific research and communication complements my data analysis work, particularly in clinical research, Real-World Evidence (RWE), and scientific communication.1I am currently developing my expertise across Healthcare Data Analysis, Clinical Data, RWE, and Medical Writing, bringing together quantitative analysis, biomedical knowledge, and the ability to communicate evidence clearly to scientific and clinical audiences.
